# NZZ readings 1983-06 to 1985-12: notes

Status: partial. 31 of 36 month ends have readings; 1983-01 to 1983-05 were not read (capacity). Only 1985-06 to 1985-12 (7 months) were read twice in separate crops
(method `eye`). 1983-06 to 1985-05 are single readings (method `eye-single`) and must not be used as verified. Swiss table not found for 1983-12 and 1984-12.

## Format of the files
The PDFs of this period are NOT Flate-compressed raw images as in the brief but one JPEG (DCTDecode, RGB, about 3650 x 5400 px) per page. `scan.mjs` fails on them
("incorrect header check"); a Python/Pillow tool (cut the JPEG stream out of the PDF by the offsets of the node index, decode, crop) worked.
Issues have 32 to 112 pages.

## Table layout 1983-1985
- Page headed "Schweizer Boersen" (page 22 to 30 depending on the issue; 24/25 in most). Printed in running text, no columns: `Name Kategorie (Vortag) print print ... R print ...`.
  The bracketed number is the previous day's price. There is no "Schluss" column. Marks: `t`, `tt`, `ttt` forward/term deals, `dt` Dividendentitel/transaction notice, `L` print concluded in the call, `R` marks the start of the second session.
  Rule used: the last print without a t-mark. Where the very last print has an `L` it was taken as it stands (note says so). This is a judgement call; the previous-day figure of the next
  issue was NOT used to double-check (only one issue per month was downloaded).
- Columns on the left strip: Transport, Banken (SBG, SBV, SKA, SVB), Finanz; right strip: Industrie (Ciba-Geigy, Nestle, Sandoz...). Headed "Zuerich, von J. Vontobel & Co., Bankiers" and "Kurse vom <date>".
- Foreign: two pages "Auslandboersen" (Frankfurt/Wien/Mailand/Amsterdam/Tokio with Vortag/Schluss columns; New York list "New Yorker Boersenkurse vom <date>", columns Vortag? Eroeffnung/Hoch/Tief/Schluss etc.; I used the Tagesschluss/"Schl." column). Frankfurt is printed with Daimler-Benz, BMW, Siemens as `Vortag Schluss`.
- 1984-05 and 1984-04: Frankfurt/Zuerich closed (Ascension/1 May); Frankfurt single price column read, date assumed (header not read), see notes in rows.

## Share classes and mapping
SBG -> ubs (I, N, PS), SBV -> sbv (I printed without class, N, PS), SKA -> credit-suisse (one line "SKA Inh. inkl. PS" and "Na. inkl. PS"), Swissair Inh./Na. -> swissair I/N, Nestle Inh./Na./PS (PS partly read),
Ciba-Geigy Inh./Na./PS, Sandoz Inh./Na./PS (the bearer Sandoz line is absent on some days). Roche was not read. Industrial Bank of Japan ("Industrial Bank" in the Tokio list)
was read only from 1984-... in a few issues (rows exist where present; the 1985 months mostly). Schlumberger and IBM from the New York list.

## Controls
- IBM printed / Yahoo (split adjusted): constant 4.18 over all 31 months (range 4.167 to 4.210; the spread is Yahoo's rounding and 1/8 steps). Passes.
- Nestle vs Journal de Geneve 1985 (bearer and registered, 22 pairs): 21 equal exactly. 1985-11: NZZ 8110 / 4475, JdG 8125 / 4400. The NZZ "Vortag" bracket in the same issue is 8125 / 4400, equal to the JdG value,
  so the JdG row labelled 29.11 shows 28.11 (JdG issue is Saturday 30.11.). The NZZ value is the 29.11. close. PS 1985-12 1600 equals JdG 1600.
- Frankfurt vs JdG print (rows "JdG print" in bmw/siemens/mercedes-raw.csv), NOT exact:
  1983-06 BMW 384=384, Daimler 569=569, Siemens 347 vs 346.5; 1984-06 BMW 399 vs 400.5, Daimler 578.5 vs 578, Siemens 394.5 vs 394; 1985-03 BMW 375 vs 378, Daimler 656 vs 662, Siemens 522 = 522;
  1985-12 BMW 570 vs 569.5, Daimler 1246 vs 1245, Siemens 755 vs 755.5. Unresolved: differences of up to 6 DM (Daimler 1985-03) mean either JdG and NZZ print different Frankfurt columns
  (Schluss vs amtlicher Kurs) or I misread; the Frankfurt rows of this file should be treated as lower reliability than the Swiss and New York rows.
  The Vortag figures in the NZZ do not systematically explain the JdG values.
- IBM 1985-12: issue of 31.12.1985 printed 158 1/4 for 30.12. (a trading day), but the issue of 3.1.1986 Vortag gives 155 for 31.12.; the 31.12. value (155, ratio 4.171) is used.

## Breaks looking like splits or class changes
None found in the readings within this period except the Nestle PS being a separate line (not a break). Nestle bearer/registered ratio about 1.9 to 2.2 stable.
Year high/low columns do not exist in this table, so no restated-range evidence was available.

## Sanity checks (month-to-month)
Not run systematically (stopped for capacity); spot checks of bearer series (Nestle I 3990 in 1983-09 to 8925 in 1985-12, SBG I 3140 to 5240, Swissair I 845 to 1790) show no factor-2 jumps; Swissair I and SBG I rise steadily.
A formal pass over all series is still to do.

## Double reading
Months 1985-10 to 1985-12 and 1985-06 to 1985-09: values read in a first pass and again from separate crops (Zurich lines from two differently cut crops). Disagreements found and resolved on re-reading:
Nestle PS Nov 1985 (initially 1500, 1510 on re-read), UBS I Nov 1985 (4975 -> 4965), Sandoz N Dec 1985, several "L" tail prints; all resolved by higher magnification. Months 1983-06 to 1985-05 were read once.

## Problems and left out
- 1983-01 to 1983-05: not read.
- 1983-12 (issue 31.12.1983) and 1984-12 (issues 31.12.1984 and 3.1.1985): no Swiss share table located in the page strips examined; only the foreign lists were read (BMW/Daimler/Siemens/IBM/Schlumberger 1983-12, IBM/Schlumberger 1984-12).
- Many Zuerich lines in 1983-84 are long; lines whose end was cut by the crop edge were read with a second crop only where noted.
- Siemens is missing in some months of 1983-84 where the row was cut off; Nestle PS and IBJ only partly read; Roche not read.
- 1985-05 Daimler: Vortag printed 701 but Schluss 825 and next month Vortag 833: plausible (Daimler-Benz rights/price move) but the 701 looks like a misprint or misread; not used.
- Tail-print selection ("last print without t-mark") is subjective in busy lines (Nestle, Ciba-Geigy in 1984); these are the most error-prone entries.

## Error rate (honest estimate)
Verified months (1985-06..12) probably 2 to 4 percent of values wrong by a few units in the last digit (tail print choice), Frankfurt rows more. Unverified months: perhaps 5 to 10 percent.
