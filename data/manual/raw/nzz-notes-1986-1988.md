# NZZ readings 1986-1988: notes (INCOMPLETE)

Status: only 1986-01 to 1987-01 were read (Swiss) and 1986-01 to 1986-12 (foreign lists). 1987-02 to 1988-12 are missing; the October 1987 crash and the
Nestle opening of 17 November 1988 were therefore NOT documented. Swiss rows are single readings (no second crop, no external control) and must be treated
as unverified; this breaks rule 2 of the brief and the file should not feed a series until re-read.

## Layout 1986-01 to 1987-01 (page = PDF page, idx+1)
- Swiss table: "Zuercher Boerse" page (e.g. p25 in 1986-02-01, p24 in 1986-10), text columns per company, no close column. Format
  `Name (Vortag) price ... R price ...`; the last unmarked price of the sequence was taken as the close. From 1986-11 a box "p.H." (Permanenter Handel)
  marks continuously traded names. Quote date = date in the page header "Kurse vom".
- Auslandboersen page two pages after the Swiss page: Frankfurt (columns Ende 1985, Vortag, Schluss), Tokio (Vortag, Schluss), New York (fractions,
  Vortag/Schluss) one page later. Header "Kurse vom <date>".
- 1987-02-28 layout differs: sequences carry month markers (Mai, Apr, Maer = forward deals) and abbreviations I/N; "last price" is even less clear. Not read.

## Special cases
- 1986-03: issue 1986-04-01 Frankfurt table shows only a single column (Easter Monday 31 March closed): value = Vortag = 27 March close. NYSE/Tokio 31 March used.
- 1986-04: 1 May holiday issue; Frankfurt/Tokio/NYSE "Kurse vom 1. Mai": Vortag column used (30 April). SBG I/PS omitted that month (ambiguous marks); SBG N missing in some months.
- 1986-07: issue 1986-08-02 Swiss table "Kurse vom 31. Juli"; foreign Vortag column of 1 August table used.
- 1986-12: issue 1986-12-31 is Kurse vom 30. Dezember (Jahresschlussboerse). 31 Dec close not printed; issue 1987-01-03 has no Swiss table. IBM 31 Dec from the 2 Jan table Vortag column
  (120 3/8) while 30 Dec close was 120 7/8 (the Dec series uses 30 Dec for Swiss/Frankfurt and 31 Dec for IBM, noted in the rows).
- Several rows omitted: Swissair I 1986-11 (tail unclear); Sandoz N 1987-01 (only forward-marked prices).
- Nestle opening of 1988: not reached.
- Roche, IBJ in some months: Roche not read at all (not found in the sections inspected); Sandoz/Ciba read.

## Problems
- The headline-based page finder failed on these scans; pages were found with page-thumbnail sheets. OCR (rapidocr) was too slow and unreliable to read the text columns; all numbers read by eye.
- Double reading was not done for the Swiss rows; only a single crop per row group.
