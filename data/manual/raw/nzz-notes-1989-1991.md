# NZZ month-end readings 1989-01 to 1991-12: notes

35 of 36 months are read (825 rows in `nzz-readings-1989-1991.csv`). Missing: 1990-12 (see Problems).

## Files and tools
The issues of this period are JPEG-per-page scans (not Flate as the brief said). `scan.mjs` crashed with "incorrect header check"; I used a Python/Pillow copy that reads the same page-image index. Pages are 3500 to 3700 px wide.

## Table layouts
- 1989 to January 1990 (issues of 1 Feb 1989 to 1 Feb 1990): page "ZÜRCHER BÖRSE", section "Aktienmarkt Schweiz", header "Kurse vom <date>". Each share is one text entry: previous close in round brackets, then the day's ticks (R = Reprise, L = Losgeschäft, a month suffix such as Mai/Jun = forward trade, [..] = Applikationskurs, V = Volumen). No closing-price column. Close = last plain cash tick. Confirmed against the Soffex box (underlying "Basiswert" with its Tagesschlusskurs) for Nestlé I, Ciba I, Sandoz PS, SBG I, SBV I, SKA/CS I, Roche GS/I, Swissair I (when listed). Pages 39 to 44 of the Zürich part, Frankfurt/Tokio/New York on the next three pages.
- From February 1990 (issue 1 Mar 1990; the 1 Feb issue still has ticks only): same page with a box of the most traded shares, columns Jahres-H, Jahres-T, Vortag, Eröffnung, H, T, Volumen Stück, Volumen 1000 Fr., Tagesschluss. I used Tagesschluss. From the issue of 2 May 1990 (April) only box shares are read for the Swiss list: Swissair I, SBG I/N/PS, SBV I/N/PS, CS Holding I/N, Nestlé I/N/PS, Sandoz N/PS (I from July 1991), Ciba I/N/PS, Roche GS (I from Aug 1991 to Nov 1991, and from 1989-10 to 1990-03 from the tick table). Swissair N and Swissair GS are not in the box and drop out; SBG PS, SBV N, CS N, Sandoz I, Roche I enter and leave the box.
- Foreign pages: Frankfurt, Tokio and New York, each with Vortag, Tages-H, Tages-T, Tagesschluss (NY: plus volume and P/E). Tokyo list shows "Industrial Bk." (IBJ), listed in all months read. Daimler-Benz is the row "Daimler-Benz".
- SKA: combined lines "SKA I+PS" and "SKA N+PS" until the issue of 1 Apr 1989; from the issue of 29 Apr 1989 "CS Holding I" and "CS Holding N" (the CS Holding PS line is no longer printed). The CS Holding line is in the continuation ("Fortsetzung von Seite ...") of the Zürich page in 1989-04 and 1989-05.
- Swissair: Swissair GS has a line from March 1989 to December 1989 and disappears in 1990-01 (not read from February 1990).
- Page numbers in the CSV are the page numbers printed on the pages as far as I identified them; for the 1989 issues some were inferred from the image index (index + 1) and may be off by one. The Zürich page of the box layout is the page headed "ZÜRCHER BÖRSE".

## Quote date handling
- The issue prints the close of the previous trading day. Zürich "Kurse vom" header read in every issue.
- Issues after 1 May / 1 August holidays (1989-07, 1990-04, 1990-07, 1991-04, 1991-07): the Zürich table is "Kurse vom 31. Juli/30. April"; the foreign tables are "Kurse vom 1. August/1. Mai" and the month-end close is the Vortag column (BMW, Daimler, Siemens, IBJ, IBM, SLB of those months). 1990-04: the Tokio table shows the Vortag as "27.4." (Tokyo closed 30 April), so IBJ is quoted at 1990-04-27 (3770); Frankfurt 1990-04 not usable (below).
- 1989-12 (issue 30 Dec 1989): Zürich, Frankfurt, Vienna, Brussels were closed on Friday 29 Dec; Zürich table is "Kurse vom 28. Dezember"; Frankfurt only has the Vortag column filled (28 Dec). The foreign Tokyo/New York tables are Kurse vom 29. Dezember. Quote dates set accordingly.
- 1991-03 (issue 30 Mar 1991, Good Friday 29 March): Zürich/Frankfurt/New York print Thursday 28 March; Tokyo was open on 29 March (IBJ quote_date 1991-03-29).
- 1991-11 (issue Sat 30 Nov): header "Kurse vom 29. November" (Friday).
- 1991-12: see Problems.

## Splits and class changes seen in the paper (not adjusted)
- Sandoz N: 11500 (1991-04, box range 11750/7910) -> 2400 (1991-05, range 2410/1543, issue 1 Jun 1991): about 1-for-5 split in May 1991. Sandoz PS continues at 2240 -> 2300 (its year range changes 2340/1750 -> 2320/1706 only). Sandoz I (not in the box until 1991-07) is 2470 on the new basis.
- Roche GS 5330 (1991-10) -> 2630 (1991-11), range 5450/3390 -> 2780/1688; Roche I 8300 -> 4200, range 8380/6210 -> 4450/3099: 1-for-2 split of both classes in November 1991 (control only).
- CS Holding I/N: year range printed 2230/1360 and 430/275 on 2 May 1991 -> 2188/1334 and 422/270 on 1 Jun 1991: small restatement, probably a rights issue/capital measure in May 1991. Prices continuous.
- Ibj: 6740 high / 3120 low printed in 1990-03: crash of the Tokyo market, no split.
- Schlumberger and IBM have no breaks; IBM x 4.184 matches Yahoo.
- SKA/CS I+PS -> I: the 1989-03 line "SKA I+PS" (2895) and 1989-04 "CS Holding I" (2620) are the same share in a new line; CS N+PS (591) -> CS N (543) likewise. The N+PS price is not comparable to the later N line (the PS was dropped). I did not adjust.

## Controls
- Frankfurt (BMW, Daimler-Benz, Siemens) against onvista daily closes x the documented factors (50.8516, 23.5586, 30.2354): 100 comparisons; 99 equal to 0.01 percent or better, one at 0.9992 (Daimler 1989-04-28: printed 669, onvista implies 669.5). The "JdG print" rows for 1990-01-31 (BMW 533, Siemens 738, Daimler 833), 1991-05-31 (570, 645, 757.5) and 1991-06-28 (473.5, 635.5, 750) are matched exactly.
- Nestlé N against Yahoo x 1000 (23 months from 1990-01): 16 exact; seven with Yahoo a few ticks off the printed close (1990-01 8480 vs 8475; 1990-02 8570 vs 8555; 1990-04 7975 vs 8000 [Yahoo equals a Losgeschäft of 8000 printed just before the closing tick]; 1990-05 8725 vs 8750; 1990-07 8280 vs 8270; 1990-09 6640 vs 6650; 1991-06 8400 vs 8380). Largest deviation 0.31 percent. Nestlé 1990-01 also agrees exactly with the JdG print (I 8745, N 8480, PS 1720).
- IBM against Yahoo: printed / Yahoo = 4.153 to 4.204 over 35 months, median 4.184 (the scatter is Yahoo's month-end date; the extreme low 4.153 is 1990-08).
- Schlumberger has no control here.

## Double reading
Disagreements between first and second reading: about 20 values; most were slips at 1x or crops that cut off a column and were fixed by recropping (e.g. Daimler/BMW row confusion with Mercedes Holding, Sandoz row offsets). No value that I recorded stands on a disagreement. One systematic trap: in the box layout the box row can differ from the tick text; where both exist (1990-04 only) they agreed except Ciba N (text 2650 vs box 2640), which I left out.

## Problems and gaps
- 1990-12: NOT READ. The issue of 31 Dec 1990 (Monday, 36 pages) has no share tables; the issue of 3 Jan 1991 is a 48-page file in which the Zürich table page is absent (the page that should carry it, and pages 29/30, are advertisements; the foreign tables are Kurse vom 2. Januar). No Swiss closing prices for end of December 1990 were found.
- 1989-03 Daimler-Benz: issue 1 Apr 1989 prints Vortag 655, H 665, T 654.50, Schluss 657; onvista 31 March close = DM 665.01, equal to the printed high. Dropped.
- 1990-04 Frankfurt (BMW, Daimler, Siemens): the table is "Kurse vom 30. April/1. Mai" and the Vortag column does not match the 30 April closes of onvista (BMW 580.50 vs 575, Daimler 825 vs 822.5, Siemens 743 vs 736). Dropped. Schlumberger 1990-04 unreadable (50 1/8 or 50 7/8). Dropped.
- 1990-05 BMW: printed Vortag 596, H 597, T 584.50, Schluss 584.50; onvista close 597.0 (equal to the printed high). Dropped.
- 1990-04 Swiss rows that are not in the box (Swissair N, SBG N, Sandoz I, CS N, Ciba N, Roche I, sandoz PS ok) are not recorded because there was no second place; Ciba N text 2650 vs box 2640.
- 1990-09 Ciba PS: no trade ("-" in the box).
- 1991-12: the issue of 31 Dec 1991 prints Kurse vom 30. Dezember. There is no issue of 1 Jan, and the issue of 3 Jan has no Zürich table (Kurse vom 2. Januar for foreign lists). Swiss shares of 1991-12 are therefore quoted at 1991-12-30 and may differ from the 31 Dec 1991 close if Zürich traded that day (Yahoo's Nestlé N for 1991-12 is 8600, the same as printed on 30 Dec). Frankfurt BMW/Daimler/Siemens and IBJ: 30 Dec (Frankfurt had no close on 31 Dec). IBM and Schlumberger: 31 Dec taken from the Vortag column of the 3 Jan issue (IBM 89, matching Yahoo 21.27 x 4.184 = 89.0; the 30 Dec close of 90 3/8 does not).
- Swissair 1989-01 to 1989-04: the "(..)" bracket after the name is the previous close; I used the last tick. Swissair 1990-05 onwards only I.
- Zürich box of 1990-02 to 1990-04: rows I did not read are not recorded.
- The first reading of Sandoz I on its post-split basis is 1991-07 (2470).

## Error rate estimate
Printed digits were read at full resolution from the scan; rows with a Soffex or box confirmation (about 60 percent of the Swiss rows) or an onvista/Yahoo control (Frankfurt, Nestlé N, IBM) show no errors apart from the cases listed. I estimate fewer than 1 percent wrong values in the Swiss rows without an independent confirmation (1989 tick sequences of Swissair N, SBG N, Sandoz I/N, Ciba N/PS, SBV PS, SBG PS, CS N), where the last-tick rule could pick a forward tick, and well under 0.5 percent elsewhere. Systematic risk: 1989 ticks with L or forward markers.
