# NZZ month-end readings, January 1992 to December 1994: notes

Companion to `nzz-readings-1992-1994.csv` (776 readings, 35 of the 36 month ends). All values were read by eye from the page scans in `data/manual/scans/nzz/` (outside git) on 2026-10-02. The scans of this period are JPEG page images (1992 to at least April 1993) and Flate images (some later issues), 3600 x 5400 pixels, no text layer. The scan tool of the pilot (Flate only) was copied and wrapped with a JPEG-capable reader; nothing in the repo was changed except the two files of this period.

## Months covered and missing

- Missing: 1994-05. The archive has no issue between 26 May and 15 June 1994 (download log).
- 1993-05: only the Tokyo row (IBJ 2880) could be read. The file NZZ_1993-06-01.pdf has only 63 page images, and the Swiss, Frankfurt and New York tables of the issue are not in it (pages 33 to 38 and 40 to 44 were looked at: Tokio on image 38, the rest is editorial or other markets). All other shares of May 1993 are therefore missing.
- Every other month has Swiss shares and Frankfurt prints; Tokyo and New York are present except where the table below says otherwise.

## Table layouts per sub-period

Page numbers in the CSV are 1-based indexes of the page image in the PDF (the printed page number is the image index in 1992 to July 1994 and image index + 1 in some later issues; the CSV uses the image index).

1. January 1992 to April 1993: the "Permanent gehandelte Schweizer Aktien" table on the Swiss stocks page (image 41 to 45 in 1992, 37 to 45 in 1993). Columns: Jahres-H, Jahres-T (year high and low), Vortag, Eroeffnung, Tages-H, Tages-T, Volumen, Tagesschluss. The close is the LAST column; the previous-day close is the Vortag column. Share classes by name: Nestle I / N / PS, SBG I / N, SBV I / N / PS, CS Holding I / N, Ciba-Geigy I / N / PS, Sandoz I / N / PS, Roche I and GS. Swissair is not in the permanent table from 1993 (earlier, Swissair I, N and the GS line sit in the "Transport" block of the text listing, where each share shows only the last prices before a "V"/"L" marker): Swissair values in 1992 to 1993-04 come from that listing (I, N, GS). A few rows are single prices marked L (letzter Kurs) and are noted as such in the CSV.
2. Foreign pages in this period: Frankfurt ("Europa" page) columns Vortag, Eroeffnung, Hoechst, Tiefst, Schluss; Tokio (Asia page) Vortag, Eroeffnung, Hoechst, Tiefst, Tagesschluss; New York (USA page) Vortag, Hoechst, Tiefst, Schluss with fractions. Header "Kurse vom <Datum>" on every page.
3. From June 1993 the permanent table keeps the same columns but Nestle shows only N (see Nestle below).
4. From March 1994 the Swiss page is headed "Schweizer Aktien", permanent table at the bottom left; columns as before.
5. From August 1994 the page is "Schweizer Boersen": alphabetical "Inland" table (Jahres H/T, Vortag, Tages H, Tages T, Volumen 1000 Fr., Schluss) with the Ausland box (USA/Kanada, Deutschland) which prints SWISS FRANC translations (e.g. Daimler 706, IBM 92): not used. Frankfurt, Tokyo and New York are read from their own pages (Europa/Suedafrika, Asien/Pazifik, USA/Kanada) in DM, yen and USD. Bold names are the permanent-trading shares.
6. December 1994: see below.

## Quote dates and holidays

- Every row's quote_date is read from the header of the table ("Kurse vom ..."), not assumed.
- 1992-12 (issue 31.12.1992, "Kurse vom 30. Dezember"): last trading day of the year. The first January issue (5.1.1993) prints 4 January; its Vortag column (= 30 December) repeats Nestle I 1145, N 1160, Ciba-Geigy I 670, N 656, PS 650 and CS Holding I 2130, N 397 from the 31 December issue (checked for these rows).
- 1993-04 (issue 4.5.1993): prints "Kurse vom 3. Mai", the first trading day of May. The month-end close is the Vortag column (30 April). Check: Frankfurt Vortag for BMW 464, Siemens 616, Daimler 575.50 equals the onvista closes of 30 April; Nestle N 1070 equals Yahoo 10.70; IBJ: the Tokyo table of 3 May has only the Vortag column filled (3 May was a Japanese holiday), so 2680 is the last Tokyo close before 3 May, presumably 30 April; the page does not print that date, so quote_date is left empty in the CSV.
- 1992-04 (issue 2.5.1992): 1 May was a holiday in Zurich; the Swiss table is of 30 April. The New York table is headed "Kurse vom 1. Mai"; the Vortag column (30 April) was taken for Schlumberger 62 1/8 and IBM 90 3/4.
- 1994-03 (issue 2.4.1994): Swiss table of 31 March. The Frankfurt table is headed "Kurse vom 31. Maerz/1. April"; 1 April was Good Friday, Frankfurt closed, values are 31 March (equal to onvista 31.3.). The Tokyo table carries the same two-day header and Tokyo traded on 1 April, so the day of the close column cannot be settled from the page: IBJ for 1994-03 left out. New York is "Kurse vom 31. Maerz".
- 1994-04 (issue 30.4.1994): IBJ 3160 is the Vortag column of the Tokyo table of 29 April (Showa Day, market closed, only Vortag filled), i.e. the close of 28 April; quote_date of that row is 1994-04-28 (inferred from the holiday).
- 1994-12: The Swiss exchanges were closed on Friday 30.12.1994 (Tagesspiegel in the issue of 31.12/1.1.1995: "Die Zuercher Boerse blieb am Freitag geschlossen"; the SPI chart shows 29 December as the last day). The issue has no daily Swiss table. The Swiss prices of the CSV are taken from the weekly table "Boersenkurse im Wochenvergleich (23.12.94 bis 29.12.94)" on image 27, column "Berichtswoche" = close of 29.12.1994. These rows have quote_date 1994-12-29, were read in one crop each and have no second source other than Nestle N against Yahoo (1247 vs 12.44, +0.24 percent). Frankfurt, Tokyo and New York of December 1994 are from the tables "Kurse vom 30. Dezember" (quote_date 1994-12-30); Frankfurt matches onvista exactly.

## Nestle: the share change (the control for the divisor)

- Until 31.8.1992 the table shows Nestle I (bearer) 9120, N (registered) 9140 and PS 1835 (Aug 1992). At the 30.9.1992 close the table shows Nestle I 976 and N 988 with the year high/low restated by a factor of ten, while the PS stays at 1930. So the 1:10 split of both Nestle shares happened between the August and September 1992 month ends (not in 1993). The registered share divided by 1000 (to 1992-08) or 100 (from 1992-09) equals Yahoo (data/raw/yahoo/nestle.csv).
- From the 31.3.1993 close the table still has Nestle I, N and PS (1190, 1190, 2390); on 30.4./3.5.1993 too (1075, 1070, 2120). In the issue for 30 June 1993 (the issue of 1.6.1993 has no tables) only a line "Nestle N" is left: 1141. So the unification of the three classes into one registered share fell between 3 May and 30 June 1993 (the paper in between was not read). Nestle N continues the line of the registered share (printed / 100 equals Yahoo; 1141 vs 11.42 in June 1993).
- Printed Nestle PS before the unification: 1775 (1992-01) to 2390 (1993-03), unaffected by the 1992 split.

## Splits and class changes seen in the paper (evidence: restated Jahres-H/T next to the quote)

- Nestle I/N 1:10 (Aug to Sep 1992), see above.
- SBG I 3570 (30.6.1992) -> 701 (31.7.1992), N 784 -> 153: 1:5, year range restated.
- Ciba-Geigy I 3320 -> 662, N 3350 -> 664, PS 3320 -> 652 (30.6. to 31.7.1992): 1:5.
- SBV N 346 (31.3.1993) -> 166 (30.4.1993): 1:2 on the registered share (year range 337/293 -> 180/147); the SBV PS line is gone from May 1993; SBV I stays at about 340.
- CS Holding I 3595 (30.11.1993) -> 737 (30.12.1993), N 708 -> 147: 1:5.
- Sandoz I 3710 (29.4.1994) -> 718 (30.6.1994), N 3720 -> 694: 1:5, PS gone. (Roche I and GS are not split in this period.)
- Ciba-Geigy PS disappears from the table in June 1994.
- Not adjusted anywhere; the legal terms of these changes are not in the issues read.

## Controls

- Nestle N vs Yahoo (printed / 1000 to 1992-08, / 100 from 1992-09): 34 comparisons; 20 exactly equal; 32 within 0.3 percent; the two others are 1992-12 (1160 vs 11.65, -0.43 percent) and 1993-02 (1100 vs 11.05, -0.45 percent). In 1992-12 the 30 December close is confirmed in the 5.1.1993 issue (Vortag 1160); Yahoo's value may stem from another day or exchange. 1993-02 has no second paper source. Largest positive deviation +0.26 percent (1994-10).
- IBM vs Yahoo (split-adjusted): printed / Yahoo is 4.184 in 22 of 29 months compared, and 4.146 to 4.230 otherwise (month-end date scatter of Yahoo), no break.
- BMW, Siemens, Daimler-Benz DM closes x onvista factors (50.8516, 30.2354, 23.5586): equal to within 0.02 percent in all 35 months (ratio 1.0000), same trading day. Includes the two-day-header months 1993-04 and 1994-03 where the previous-day column was taken.
- JdG overlap file (pilot-journal-de-geneve.csv: Swiss names 1995 only): no overlap with this period.
- Schlumberger has no Yahoo control file; the values are read from the New York table, the Vortag column and the year range were used for plausibility (no jumps above 12 percent month to month, 50 to 75 USD range).

## How the double reading went

First pass: crops of the permanent table and foreign pages for every month; second pass: new crops of shifted windows read without the first values at hand (for 1992-01 and 1992-02 the second pass contained its own header crops). Disagreements: Mercedes 1993-08 first read 756 vs second 755 (settled to 755 by onvista and a magnified crop); IBM 1994-01, 1994-02 and 1994-09 fractions could not be read with certainty (left out); Schlumberger 1993-02 left out. Rows read in a single crop are flagged in the CSV note ("read in one crop only") for Ciba-Geigy I 1993-10, Roche I and IBJ 1994-10, IBJ 1994-11, and all Swiss rows of 1994-12. Several 1993-06 to 1993-12 rows rely on two overlapping but not fully independent crops. Because Nestle N and the three German shares agree with the controls in all months, and every IBM check is constant, a wrong date or row is unlikely; the remaining risk is a digit error in a share without a control (SBG, SBV, CS, Swissair, Sandoz, Ciba, Roche, IBJ, Schlumberger).

## Unusual points and problems

- The 1993-01 and 1993-02 Swissair I values come from the text listing (single price before V; the permanent table no longer carries Swissair); in 1993-06 to 1994-12 the permanent table has Swissair N (and a Swissair GS line from 1994-10), first in the text listing then in the permanent table.
- In 1992-07 the SBG and Ciba-Geigy splits make the month-to-month ratio about 0.2; not adjusted.
- Mercedes in 1993-08 and the Daimler row sit in the "Europa" page as "Daimler-Benz"; BMW appears in the Frankfurt list in all months except 1994-08 (Europa/Suedafrika page: present; the Ausland box lacks it).
- Roche I and GS are read as controls; Roche I has a year high 13040 (1994) etc., no break.
- IBJ prices are on the Tokyo list as "Industrial Bk." (1992: "Industrial Bank of Japan"); present in all months read except 1994-03 (day unclear); 1993-04 and 1994-04 are Vortag values (holiday in Tokyo).
- IBJ 1993-04 and 1994-04 are Vortag values of tables of Japanese holidays (last Tokyo close before the month end, not a printed close of the month-end day); treat them as such.
- No attempt was made to make the series continuous: all values are raw.
