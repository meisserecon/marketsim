# NZZ readings January 1995 to February 1998: notes

Readings are in `nzz-readings-1995-1998.csv` (587 rows). Read by eye from page scans (4-bit grayscale, about 3700 x 5550 pixels) of 40 issues in `data/manual/scans/nzz/`. Nothing was entered from memory or from Yahoo. Where a number is stated as "single table reading" in the csv note column it was read once in the main table only.

## Table layouts

All Swiss quotes come from the table "Schweizer Aktien" (heading "Aktienmarkt Schweiz" / "Schweizer Börsen", later "Börsen und Märkte"), headed "Kurse vom <date>".

Columns, left to right: Jahres-H., Jahres-T. (year high, low), name, Vortag (previous day close), Tages-H., Tages-T., Volumen in Stck., Schluss (close of the day). The close is printed directly after the volume without gap (for example "2570317180" is volume 25703, close 17180; "32126000" is volume 321, close 26000), so digit counts were checked against the neighbouring columns. Prices below CHF 20 are decimals, higher ones print fractions (1/4, 1/2, 3/4) in the Vortag, Tages-H., Tages-T. and Schluss columns of the 1995 to 1997 tables. Fraction glyphs at the close are small; the 1/4 versus 3/4 and 1/2 versus 3/4 distinction was the main source of doubt (see "Double reading").

Sub-periods:
- Jan 1995 to Jun 1996: table on printed page 33 or 35 (PDF image index = page - 2), two blocks of columns per page, alphabetical, bold type for the big companies. Alphabetical order, bearer/registered/PS lines of one company adjacent ("Sandoz I", "Sandoz N", "Ciba-Geigy I", "Ciba-Geigy N", "SBG I", "SBG N", "SBV I", "SBV N", "CS Hold I/N", "Swissair N", "Roche I", "Roche GS"). A "Meistgehandelte Titel" box on the right column and a Soffex page (options and underlyings; Soffex shows values truncated, 233.7 for 233 3/4) give second sources. Frankfurt on the page "Europa/Südafrika" or "Börsen und Märkte" (printed page 37 to 39, columns Vortag, H, T, Tagesschluss), Tokio on page 39 to 41 ("Asien/Pazifik", same columns).
- Jul 1996 to Feb 1997: same table, but the Swiss table moved to pages 31 to 37 depending on the issue; from the issue of 29.6.1996 it has a second column block on the same page for the lower half of the alphabet (page 35 in 1997). Soffex page follows ("Aktienmarkt Schweiz / Soffex").
- Mar 1997 to Feb 1998: Swiss table on printed page 35 (37 in the issues of 31.12.1996/3.1.1997 and 1.4.1997), Frankfurt page 39 (38 in some), Tokio page 33 or 34. Table bold lines: Nestlé N, Novartis I/N, Roche GS, SBV N, UBS I/N, CS Group N, SAirGroup... From the issue of 3.1.1998 the table says "Verschobener Jahreswechsel": year high/low refer to 1997 until mid-January 1998, in the issue of 31.1.1998 they refer to 1998.
- Jan to May 1995 the second CS line "CS Hold I" (bearer) exists (CHF 513 to 545), it is no longer listed from the issue of 1.7.1995. SBV I (bearer) lasts to the issue of 2.5.1996 and disappears from 1.6.1996. Swissair GS appears and disappears several times (listed March, April, Aug, Nov 1995, Jan to Apr 1996, Aug 1996, Oct to Dec 1996 and again from May 1997 as "SAirGroup GS"; not listed in other months).

Frankfurt and Tokio tables are narrow single-line rows: Vortag, H., T., Tagesschluss. Tokio list is "Industrial Bk." in the alphabetical block under "I".

## Quote dates

The header "Kurse vom <date>" was read for most issues. Special cases:
- Issue of 2.8.1995, 2.8.1996, 2.8.1997 (1 August holiday): the Swiss table prints 31 July (1995, 1996, 1997 read in the header or confirmed by Nestlé = Yahoo). In 1996 and 1997 the Frankfurt and Tokio tables are headed "Kurse vom 1. August": the Vortag column (31 July) was taken (BMW, Daimler, Siemens all equal onvista 31.7.; Tokio IBJ Vortag taken, in 1997 with 1 August close 1600 against 1650).
- Issue of 2.5.1996 and 2.5.1997 (1 May): Swiss table "Kurse vom 30. April". Frankfurt "30. April / 1. Mai" in 1996 and 1997: the 30 April column taken (matches onvista). Tokio 1997: header "Kurse vom 1. Mai", Vortag column (30 April) taken, flagged in the csv; the 1 May close was 1230.
- December: 31.12.1995 was a Sunday; the issue of 30.12.1995 prints "Kurse vom 29. Dezember" for Frankfurt and Tokio (taken as December 1995). It contains no Swiss share table, so December 1995 Swiss values are missing. 1996: the issue of 31.12.1996 prints 30 December, the issue of 3.1.1997 (page 37) prints "Kurse vom 31. Dezember" for Switzerland; the Vortag column of the 3.1.1997 table equals the 30 December close (checked for Tege, Von Roll, SBG). Frankfurt and Tokio: 30 December taken from 3.1.1997 (Frankfurt, "Kurse vom 2. Januar", Vortag column, equals onvista 30.12.1996) and from the issue of 31.12.1996 (Tokio, 30 Dec). 1997: Swiss 31 December from the issue of 3.1.1998 (page 37); Frankfurt and Tokio 30 December from the issue of 31.12.1997 (page 39 and 34, "Kurse vom 30. Dezember").
- Issue of 1.4.1997 (Easter, Swiss and German exchanges closed 28 and 31 March): contains the Tokio table "Kurse vom 31. März" only (IBJ 1260) and no Swiss or Frankfurt table. The last Swiss trading day (27 March) is in the issue of 29.3.1997, which was not downloaded: March 1997 Swiss and Frankfurt are missing.

## Controls

**Nestlé N against Yahoo x 100** (36 months): 24 exact; the others differ by 1 to 4 francs (0.07 to 0.31 percent): 1995-03 1105/1104, 1995-04 1119/1121, 1995-09 1183/1184, 1995-10 1190/1189, 1995-11 1253/1254, 1996-01 1275/1279, 1996-02 1312/1311, 1996-03 1342/1344, 1996-04 1381/1380, 1996-05 1410/1412, 1996-06 1429/1430, 1996-07 1367/1363. All differences lie in Yahoo's early period and are consistent with Yahoo using a slightly different date or intraday value; the printed close and the day high/low columns are consistent in every case. Exact from 1996-08 on in all months.

**UBS (Yahoo ubs.csv):** Yahoo follows the Bankgesellschaft/UBS registered share (N, the line later named "UBS N"). Ratio printed SBG N / Yahoo: 12.08 constant in every month from September 1996 to February 1998 (28 months, range 12.03 to 12.15 with only 1997-10 at 12.15); before that it varies 11.4 to 13.1 because Yahoo's 1995 to mid-1996 values are stale (1995-11 equals 1995-12 value 21.77; 1996-02, -03, -04 all 22.93) so its early months are not month-end closes. Other lines: SBG/UBS I / Yahoo about 53 to 60 (rising to a constant 60.4 to 60.7 from 1997-04: I/N = 5.00, consistent with the nominal 100/20), SBV N / Yahoo 9.3 to 14.7 (not constant, as expected), SBV I 18.6 to 22.7. So Yahoo is the SBG N line.

**Novartis (Yahoo novartis.csv):** Yahoo tracks Sandoz N exactly before the merger: printed Sandoz N / Yahoo = 50.0 in all 19 months 1995-04 to 1996-11 (Sandoz I 49.6 to 51.0). It does not track Ciba-Geigy (ratio 47.3 to 53.3, not constant, different company) nor bearer lines. Novartis N / Yahoo = 49.96 in 1996-12 and 1997-01, then 47.74 constant from 1997-02 to 1998-02 (one month, 1997-07, 48.0). So the series is Sandoz N / 50 up to November 1996, continues on the Novartis N print / 50 in December 1996 and January 1997 (Novartis N replaced Sandoz N one to one in the printed prices), and changes its conversion factor to x 0.020946 (= 1/47.74) from February 1997: a step of 4.7 percent in the Yahoo basis between January and February 1997 (1997-01 32.60 = 1629/49.96; 1997-02 35.31 = 1686/47.74) that is not in the printed prices (1629 to 1686 is +3.5 percent). Either Yahoo's early rows are back-filled with Sandoz N or its adjustment factor is not constant; the Yahoo months before February 1997 are on a different scale than those after. Printed prices are continuous through the merger: Sandoz N 1516 (29.11.1996), Novartis N 1533 (31.12.1996).

**Frankfurt (BMW, Daimler-Benz, Siemens) against onvista:** In every month from 1996-04 on (all months with a print) the printed Tagesschluss equals onvista x factor to the last printed digit: BMW x 50.85, Daimler-Benz x 23.5586 up to 28.6.1996 and x 2.35586 from 31.7.1996, Siemens x 30.2354 to April 1996 and x 3.02354 from May 1996. Months compared: 1996-04 to 1998-02 (23 months, 69 pairs) all agree; none differs. For these months the second reading is the onvista value (derived after the read and matched; where a row was noted, the match is stated in the csv note). 1995-01 to 1996-03 Frankfurt prints were read twice in crops; they were not tabulated against onvista in this session, checked here at random: Daimler 1995-01 695 DM / 23.5586 = 29.50 (onvista 29.5012), BMW 1995-02 758 / 50.85 = 14.906 (14.9061), Siemens 1995-03 647.5 / 30.2354 = 21.416 (21.4155), Daimler 1996-03 803 / 23.5586 = 34.086 (34.0855): all equal.

**Journal de Genève prints (31.8.1995 and 31.10.1995, `pilot-journal-de-geneve.csv`):** all 20 values equal the NZZ values: Aug: SBG I 1075, SBG N 235, SBV I 410, SBV N 205.75, Swissair N 760, CS Hold N 98.75, Sandoz I 882, N 869, Ciba I 855, N 853; Oct: SBG I 1230, N 260, SBV I 466, N 232.75, Swissair N 715. In Oct 1995 the NZZ fraction of SBV N (232 1/4 or 3/4) could not be told apart in two crops; the JdG print 232.75 was used to decide, so that single value is not independent evidence for the JdG agreement.

**IBM and Schlumberger (NYSE list):** not recorded. The US list prints prices in fractions with a different small font; at 1:1 scan resolution the fraction digits are indistinct (checked in the issue of 1.2.1995, 3x enlargement and per-pixel dump). Integer parts alone (IBM 72 in January 1995) are not sufficient for a control against Yahoo. No US values are in the csv, no IBM control was made.

## Corporate events documented in the paper

**Sandoz, Ciba-Geigy, Novartis.** Last Sandoz and Ciba-Geigy quotes in the issues read: 29 November 1996 (issue of 30.11.1996): Sandoz I 1518, N 1516; Ciba-Geigy I 1611, N 1613 (listed on page 35 in the "S" and "C" blocks together with Novartis-option underlyings; no note of the merger in the table). The issue of 31.12.1996 (Swiss table, 30 December) and of 3.1.1997 list neither Sandoz nor Ciba-Geigy: the S block runs Sarasin, Sasea Hold, Saurer, SBG I, SBG N, SBV N, Schindler; the C block runs Canon, Richemont, Cementia, Christ, CI Com, Clariant, Coop. Issues between 30.11.1996 and 31.12.1996 are not in the set (the last trading days of both companies lie in December 1996, the exact date was not seen). In the issue of 3.1.1997 the table lists "Novartis I" 1532 (Vortag 1529) and "Novartis N" 1533 (Vortag 1530), 31 December 1996, (the same price level as the predecessors); the first Novartis table quotes I read are those of 31 December 1996 (the issue of 31.12.1996, which prints 30 December, was read only for the Sandoz/Ciba absence and not for Novartis, so the first Novartis day in the table was not established). Ciba and Sandoz Soffex underlyings ("Ciba Geigy N", "Sandoz N") are present in all Soffex pages to November 1996. A new quote line "Ciba SC Hld. N" (Ciba Specialty Chemicals, 111 to 186 CHF) and "Clariant N" (Sandoz chemicals) appear in the table in 1997 (the Ciba SC line from the issue of 1.7.1997 at the latest; its first appearance was not located). The Sandoz/Ciba-Geigy merger announcement of March 1996 (Ciba-Geigy I 1475 on 29.3.1996 against 1067 on 29.2.1996, Sandoz I 1384 against 1126) shows as a jump of 38 percent for Ciba and 23 percent for Sandoz in the March 1996 readings; no notice or exchange terms were read.

**Daimler-Benz 10:1 split.** The Frankfurt prints are 814.5 DM on 28 June 1996 (issue of 29.6.1996, Tagesschluss, year range printed as pre-split) and 78.37 DM on 31 July 1996 (issue of 2.8.1996). So the table lists the post-split price in the issue read for July 1996; the 1 July 1996 split date and the factor 10 are not printed in the issues read, the factor follows from onvista (x 23.5586 before, x 2.35586 after; the DM price at the split date is cut to a tenth). The year high/low columns of the post-split issues were not examined.

**Siemens 1:10 split.** 838.3 DM (30.4.1996, issue of 2.5.1996) and 85.58 (31.5.1996, issue of 1.6.1996). Both splits are seen only as quotes (factor 10 matches onvista), no notice and no year-range check.

**Names.** The SBG lines are named "UBS I"/"UBS N" from the issue of 2.5.1997 (30.4.1997 close) and "UBS N20" appears in the box in June 1997 (the registered share with nominal 20); CS Hold is "CS Group N" from the issue of 3.1.1997; Swissair is "SAirGroup N" from the issue of 31.5.1997. SBV N jumps from 409.5 (28.11.1997) to 454 (31.12.1997) in the weeks of the UBS/SBV merger announcement (8 December 1997); the I/N ratio of UBS stays 5.00, so the merger is not a quote break within this period. No merger notice was read.

**Roche, bearer and GS.** No break in either line in the period. In the issue of 31.5.1996 a third line "Ro. GS m.J.O." (GS with warrants, 9590) is also listed.

## Breaks, jumps and sanity checks

Month to month factor 2 or 10 changes appear only at the Siemens and Daimler splits. Other large moves: Ciba/Sandoz March 1996 (merger announcement), Swissair 1996-01 to 1996-02 (998 to 1155, +16 percent): the Vortag and year-range columns are consistent with the closes, so no class mix-up. Swissair GS line (130 to 350) and CS bearer line were not confused with the N lines (checked via year high/low). Warrants and options were avoided: all values come from the "Schweizer Aktien" table, none from "Optionsscheine" or Soffex (only used as second reading, where the Soffex page prints "underlying" closing prices).

## Double reading

Of the 587 rows, those from January 1995 to about April 1996 have two table crops (or a table crop plus box); later rows have a table crop plus box, Soffex underlying or onvista where one exists, and are otherwise single table readings (so marked in the csv note). I did not keep an exact count of double against single readings. Disagreements between a first and a second reading that I remember and resolved by a third zoom or a second source (not a complete list): SBV I 1995-03 (373 against 378, the Vortag), UBS N 1995-04 (248 against 246), CS N 1995-06 (105 1/4 against 105 1/2), CS N 1996-08 (124 1/4 against 124 1/2, resolved by the Soffex underlying 124.5), CS N 1997-06 (187 1/4 versus 187 1/2, box 187.50), SBV N 1996-11 (1/2 versus 3/4 resolved by Soffex 257.5), SBV N 1995-10 (3/4 or 1/4, resolved by JdG), UBS N 1996-12 (235.5 or 235.75). Likely error sources: fraction glyphs (see above). My honest estimate of the error rate: about 1 to 2 percent of the rows may be wrong, mostly a fraction (1/4 against 3/4, 0.25 to 0.5 CHF) on single-reading rows; whole-number misreads are expected well below 1 percent given the exact Nestlé, UBS N, Novartis and Frankfurt controls.

## Layout traps encountered

Year-end issues (December 1995 without Swiss table; "Verschobener Jahreswechsel" in January 1998), the Easter issue of 1.4.1997, two-day Frankfurt/Tokio headers on 1 May and 1 August, wrapped rows (CS Group N 31.1.1998 with volume on the first line, close on the second), volumes glued to the close, bold row names, and the changing page numbers.
