# NZZ month-end readings: holes filled (Sandoz bearer, December 1989, Industrial Bank of Japan, dropped values)

Output: `nzz-readings-holes.csv` (14 Sandoz rows, 2 IBJ rows, 7 rows from the dropped-value list, 2 Swissair rows). Tools: Python/Pillow crops of the JPEG page images (copies of the earlier agents' tools in the scratch directory `holes2`), RapidOCR only to locate lines on a page, never to read values.
Rule: every value read in two passes with fresh crops of different geometry (pass 2 without the pass 1 numbers in view); `eye3` where a convention had to be settled by evidence in the same paper.

## 1. Sandoz bearer, April 1990 to June 1991 (14 rows; December 1990 left to the other agent)
- Layout: the page "Zürcher Börse" ("Permanent gehandelte Titel", Kurse vom ...) has, besides the box with Tagesschluss, running text per company: `Sandoz I (previous close) ticks ... V (volume) N pH (...) ... PS pH (...)`. The bearer entry is directly above the registered entry. Marks: L Losgeschäft, Mai/Jun/Aug/Sep/Dez/Mär forward month, [..] Applikationskurs, V volume follows. Close = last tick without forward-month suffix or brackets; ticks marked L are kept (1990-07 ends on 11000L).
- 14 of 14 values: pass 1 and pass 2 agree. All bracket values (previous day) fit the first ticks of the line.
- Series (old basis): Apr 90 10600, May 11650, Jun 12000, Jul 11000, Aug 9350, Sep 8600, Oct 8900, Nov 8870, Jan 91 10150, Feb 10900, Mar 11500, Apr 11700. New basis: May 91 2520, Jun 91 2300.
- BASIS CHANGE: in the issue of 2 May 1991 (30 April) the line is `Sandoz I (11800) 11700 11800Mai 11700 R 11725 11700 V` (old basis, close 11700, N 11500 in the box). In the issue of 1 June 1991 (31 May) the paper prints `Sandoz I Anr 1 335 R 360 365 355 V` (the subscription right Anrecht, rights issue / split operation) followed by `I neu (2440) 2450 2460 R 2530 ... 2520 V`: the label "neu" and the bracket 2440 (30 May) show the new basis already on 31 May. So the bearer share changes basis in May 1991: April 1991 old (11700), May 1991 new (2520); registered N moved at the same time (11500 -> 2400, other agent). Ratio 11700 / 2520 = 4.6; with the rights price the paper's own box restates N 11500 -> 2310 (30 May) i.e. 1-for-5 plus market move. I record exactly what is printed, no adjustment. June 1991 `Sandoz I neu (2310) 2300 ...` confirms.
- Cross-check against N and PS of the same issues (box values in nzz-readings-1989-1991.csv): bearer/N ratio 1.00 to 1.12 over 1990 (e.g. May 90 11650/10900, Jun 12000/12000, Sep 8600/7980), 1.18 in Feb 91 (10900/9830), 1.0-1.2 in Mar/Apr 91; after the change 2520/2400 and 2300/2290.

## 4. Swissair bearer
- Jan 1986 (issue 1986-02-01): `Swissair Inh. (1900) 1860 80tttL 75tt 50 40 60 70 80 R 900 ttt 10tt`: 1880 (the six plain ticks end with 80 before R; both second-session ticks are forward). Not ambiguous; recorded 1880.
- Nov 1986 (issue 1986-11-29, p.H. box): `(1320) 1330 † 40tt 45tt 40 35 40 35 40 50 45 40 35L 50 60tt 60 † 50 60 † 70 tt 80ttt`, no R. If marks follow the figure (as everywhere else on the page) the last plain tick is 50 = 1350; if the isolated † belonged to the next figure it would be 1360. Recorded 1350, medium confidence; both values equal the earlier agent's.

## 5. Other dropped values
- Nestlé I 1981-07 (issue 1981-08-03): `(3135) 3150 R 3150 TZ 313` then `Na.`: recorded 3150. "TZ" is not explained anywhere in the paper; the digits after it are three-digit figures (not Nestlé prices; 313 equals the first digits of the bracket 3135 / in Dec 1982 382 equals the first digits of a trade 3825). I treat it as an annotation after the last price. Risk: if TZ marks something that changes the close, the value is wrong; low.
