# Life of the time: from the Walkman to ChatGPT, seen from a Swiss living room

Span: December 1979 to today.

## The story
This is the colour arc. Nothing in it is a must for the game, but without it a year reads like a
list of interest rates. The beats are chosen so that each generation of a Swiss family finds
something: the teenagers their gadgets, games and films; the parents the music and the changes in
daily routine; the grandparents the ski races, the votes and the royal weddings.

The gadgets give the arc its spine, and several belong to companies in the game: Sony's Walkman
(1980), the CD (1983) and the PlayStation (1994 to 2013); IBM's PC (1981); Apple's Macintosh
(1984), iMac (1998), iPod (2001), iTunes (2003), iPhone (2007), App Store (2008), iPad (2010) and
Vision Pro (2023); Microsoft's Windows 95; Nokia's Communicator (1996); Disney's parks, films and
streaming. Nintendo's Game Boy and Wii, Netflix, Google and Twint's predecessor apps are colour only
(Twint is tagged `ubs` because of UBS's part in Paymit). The web is told as a Swiss story, from
CERN's release in 1993.

Swiss daily life runs alongside: the Zurich youth riots (1980), the clock-face railway timetable
(1982), the Swatch (1983), the motorway vignette (1985), the army vote (1989), the S-Bahn (1990),
women's suffrage in Appenzell (1990) and the two women's strikes (1991, 2019), Lothar (1999),
the euro on three sides (2002), Expo.02, the 2003 heat record, Bahn 2000, the strong franc (2011),
the Gotthard Base Tunnel (2016), and the pandemic from lockdown to certificate (2020 to 2022).

Sport is the most reliable family glue: the ski triumphs of Sarajevo, Crans-Montana and Calgary,
Hingis, then Federer from his win over Sampras in 2001 to his retirement in 2022, Wawrinka and the
Davis Cup, Alinghi, Ammann, and the football summers from 1994 (Hodgson's team) to the World Cup
quarter-final of 2026.

The 1980s colour items already in `data/news/` are reused where they fit (Lennon, Thriller, the
first cell-phone network, Band Aid, Back to the Future, Maradona, the Game Boy, Seoul). They are
American-heavy; the arc adds the Swiss side.
