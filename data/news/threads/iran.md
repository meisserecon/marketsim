# Thread: iran — from ally to enemy, and the people in between

Span: December 1979 to today.

In 1953 a coup organised by the CIA and British intelligence toppled Prime Minister Mosaddegh,
who had nationalised Iran's oil, and strengthened the rule of the Shah, America's ally for the next
quarter century. The revolution of 1979 overthrew the Shah, and the Islamic Republic that
followed became America's most persistent enemy in the region: the game opens with American
diplomats held hostage in Tehran and Iranian oil off the US market. Iraq's invasion in 1980 starts
an eight-year war that kills hundreds of thousands and ends only after the downing of an Iranian
airliner by an American warship; even then the two enemies dealt in secret (Iran-Contra). After
Khomeini's death, Iranians twice vote for change, Khatami in 1997 and Rouhani in 2013, and twice
take to the streets when it is denied (1999, 2009), while the discovery of the nuclear sites in
2002 brings UN sanctions, a currency collapse that hits ordinary families, and finally the 2015
deal. America's withdrawal in 2018 starts the slide back: protests in 2022 under "Woman, Life,
Freedom", the twelve-day war of 2025, the uprising and massacre of January 2026, and the American
and Israeli war of February 2026 that kills Khamenei and closes the Strait of Hormuz, sending oil
above 100 dollars. The thread should let players see both the regime's conflict with the world
and the Iranians who keep trying to shape their own country; oil and markets are named where
they moved (1979-80 oil shock, 2012 sanctions, 2015 deal, 2026 Hormuz).
