# Thread: soviet — the arms race and the end of the Soviet bloc

Span: December 1979 to December 1991.

The game opens with Soviet troops in Kabul and detente in ruins. Over the next four years the
confrontation hardens: Poland's free trade union Solidarity is crushed under martial law, Reagan
calls the Soviet Union "the focus of evil" and promises a missile shield in space, and NATO's
Pershing and cruise missiles arrive in Europe while Moscow walks out of the arms talks. Behind the
front, the Soviet system is failing: three old leaders die within two and a half years, and in
March 1985 the 54-year-old Gorbachev takes over. What follows is a thaw told summit by summit
(Geneva, the near miss of Reykjavik, the INF treaty that removes the missiles of 1983), with
Chernobyl showing a secretive state that can no longer hide its failures and the retreat from
Afghanistan showing that Moscow will no longer hold on by force. In 1989 eastern Europe walks out
of the bloc, the Wall opens and Germany unites within a year; the hardliners' coup of August 1991,
meant to save the union, destroys it, and on 25 December 1991 the red flag comes down over the
Kremlin. Players should feel the fear of the early 1980s before they see it dissolve.
